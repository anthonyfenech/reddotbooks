ANTHONY FENECH

Anthony Fenech is a sportswriter and a member of the Baseball Writers' Association of America. He spent five seasons, from 2015 through 2020, as the Detroit Tigers beat writer for the Detroit Free Press, where he began in 2007, and was a top-10 finalist in the Associated Press Sports Editors awards for beat writing in 2017 and for best digital package in 2020. OFF-THE-RECORD, published by Red Dot Press, is his first book.
